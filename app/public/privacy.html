Privacy notice

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Your models stay in your browser

Projects you create or open in the EdgeRules Modeler are stored only in your browser (IndexedDB). They are never uploaded. The rules engine runs locally in WebAssembly.

Page analytics

To learn which pages are used, we count page views with PostHog, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). The analytics is cookieless: nothing is stored on or read from your device — no cookies, no local storage. So no consent banner is shown.

  • What is collected: the page address (project and element names are removed — every project page is recorded as /project/:project), the referring page, browser and operating system type, screen size, approximate country, and the time of the visit.
  • What is not collected: your models, clicks, keystrokes, screen recordings, or your name or e-mail. Your IP address is used only to derive the country and an anonymous visitor hash, and is then discarded. The hash uses a salt that changes every day, so visits on different days cannot be linked.
  • Purpose and legal basis: improving the Modeler; legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Retention: analytics events are kept for at most 12 months.
  • Processor: PostHog Inc., under a data processing agreement, with data stored in the EU.
  • Opting out: turn on “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control in your browser, and no events are sent. Blocking eu.i.posthog.com also works.

Other third parties

Fonts are served from this site; no request goes to Google Fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address to deliver the pages (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access, erasure or restriction, object to the processing, and complain to a data protection authority. Because the data is anonymous and not linked to you, we usually cannot identify events as yours. Contact: open an issue.
