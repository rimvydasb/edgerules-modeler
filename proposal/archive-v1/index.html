A mathbook with a sodas

Library, examples, workspace, top bar and project home — redrawn in the language of the Boxed and Flow editors, with the structure of a school textbook and the straw thread of Lithuanian sodai.

§1

What I saw on the current pages

Home /

  • A 700px strip in a wide window; the bottom half is empty
  • No identity: no mark, no name, no sentence about what this is
  • Projects are cards with identical black icons and “(2)”, “(3)”
  • No industries section (spec has one)

Examples

  • Header block repeats the black icon square of the Shell
  • 11px descriptions, 10px meta: hard to read
  • Three action cells per card (+ an icon-only ⓘ)
  • Level colour on the icon is a hidden code

Workspace

  • Loud yellow banner + orange “not persistent” chip for the same fact
  • “New project” card is split in two different-looking halves
  • A full-width “Open” row on each card, while the card itself is not clickable

Top bar & project home

  • Two bars, the mark twice; a browser-style ✕ tab
  • Project home is generic cards on the dot canvas — the README is the only text that tells a story
  • “Service methods” and “Workbooks” look like the same thing

Figures are the most expensive idea: each example needs a small drawing. They could be generated from layout.json of the example (the Flow Editor already knows node positions and kind colours) — a static SVG at release time in edgerules-examples.

All pages are static HTML. They share assets/ds.css and assets/ds.js; the moon button switches dark mode.
