Mortgage Origination

Abstract. Scores, checks, prices and decides a joint mortgage application. Every applicant gets a scorecard grade and a policy check; the household is graded by its weaker applicant, the loan is priced by grade and loan-to-value, the payment is stress-tested, and the bank approves, refers or declines the application, with an offer or a counter-offer.
